📸 After-Class Routine ▾
1
Photo your handwritten notes right after class
2
Open ChatGPT on Chromebook, upload your photo
3
Use ChatGPT as a tutor — ask it to explain what you don't get, not just summarise. It can get things wrong, so always double-check key facts before trusting them.
4
Paste into your class Google Doc — add today's date as a header
5
NotebookLM auto-syncs — open it to chat with your notes
6
Add any new vocab to your Quizlet set for that class
7
Drop upcoming due dates into Google Tasks while you remember
🤖 ChatGPT is a tutor, not a shortcut — use it to understand the material, not to do the work for you.
